HYDERABAD — The next phase of the Hyderabad Metro Rail has received the go-ahead, opening the way for new corridors that will link the Old City and the airport to the existing network. Officials said work on detailed project reports is complete and tenders will be floated in the coming weeks.

The expansion is expected to cut travel times sharply for commuters who currently depend on crowded roads and buses. City planners say the new lines will also ease pressure on some of Hyderabad's most congested junctions.

## What changes for commuters

Residents of the Old City, who have long waited for metro connectivity, welcomed the news. "It will save me nearly an hour every day," said a shopkeeper near Charminar. Traders' associations said better transport could bring more visitors to the area's historic markets.

> "A city of Hyderabad's size needs a transport network that grows with it."

Officials said construction will be phased to limit disruption, with traffic diversions announced well in advance. The Deccan Times will follow the project closely and bring you updates as work begins.
